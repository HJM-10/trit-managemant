## architecture-left

Use case: illustration-story. Create a production decorative website edge illustration based on the approved reference image. Input image is a STYLE REFERENCE, not a target to reproduce as a full website. Output ONLY one narrow tall architectural drawing asset with transparent background, portrait 1:3 aspect ratio. Match the lovely delicate hand-drawn British house elevations on the extreme margins of the reference: precise ink linework in muted navy and slate blue, pale ice-blue watercolor shading, small copper pipe joints, a few fine climbing plant leaves. The architecture should be richly detailed, airy and elegant, with sash windows, brick coursing, roof tiles and chimneys. Make the blue exterior downpipe a coherent vertical motif. No photographs, no UI, no lettering, no logo, no text, no frame, no drop shadow, no people. Leave all non-illustrated areas genuinely transparent. Leave rightmost 15 percent mostly clear and naturally feather the sketch out towards the RIGHT, where website text will sit. House fills left 70 percent; no isolated miniature whole house.
